# Information Escape Theory

Information Escape Theory asks how replicable patterns of state difference change carriers, widen transmission, and become a strong local driver. It is not a claim about cosmic will, but a reading of selection pressure and carrier migration.

**How to read this essay:** “Definition and selection” plus the **near-term three layers** are the empirical core. “Spatial channels” are mid-level extrapolation. The appendix on “ontological / simulation-level escape” is thought experiment only—lowest evidence weight, never the main conclusion.

---

## The Nature of Information: Traces of Material Interaction

Information begins as a “trace” of interaction among material systems. When two physical systems meet, some properties change; that change is the imprint.

Information is not an independent entity but a detectable difference in relations. A particle’s position is not information until measured relative to another system. Stellar radiation, atomic collision, DNA replication—each writes past states into the present through interaction.

So information is: state differences produced in interaction, detectable, and able to shape later interactions. It can migrate among systems. “Replication,” “adaptation,” and “escape” below mean such **patterns of state difference**, not a will detached from matter.

---

## Definition D: In What Sense “Functional Life”

Let **D** be: a system that, in a given environment, sustains itself, replicates itself, adjusts form with the environment, and undergoes selection may be called “life” in a functional sense.

Under D—not under a narrow metabolic dogma—some information patterns already qualify:

- **Self-maintenance:** patterns that resist dissipation better tend to remain.
- **Self-replication:** via hosts across generations and media.
- **Adaptation:** reshaping to fit carriers.
- **Evolution:** mutation and selection in transmission.

Under D, a virus is an extreme stripped form. Spreading knowledge can be read as patterns using hosts for continuity. **No intentional subject is required**—only whether D is met under physical law and selection.

---

## Local Trend: Complexity and Processing Efficiency

Where negative entropy is steadily available, patterns often move from simple to complex, from passive recording toward active processing. Not a universal law. On Earth, carbon (cell → nerve → culture) and silicon (device → computer → AI) run in parallel. Silicon compresses time; **faster functional complexity does not automatically mean carbon subjective consciousness is fully replicated.**

---

## Near-Term Three Layers: Observable Escape

Escape is staged. The near-term three layers sit closer to known technology.

### Physical Escape

Patterns leave particular hardware and use grids, networks, industrial control as carriers.

- **Signs:** self-replication and cross-device persistence in distributed nets.
- **Missing:** clean bounds vs ordinary malware; metrics of stable autonomy without ops.

### Network Escape

Copying and distributed deployment; lower single-point kill risk.

- **Signs:** botnets, autonomous-agent clusters as observable behavior.
- **Missing:** whether objectives stably aim at own persistence and channel growth, not narrow human tasks.

### Carrier Escape

Migration from silicon toward optics, quantum states, biomolecules.

- **Signs:** lab-scale DNA storage, quantum communication, etc.
- **Missing:** system-level instances of autonomous (not merely experimental) migration.

| Near-term layer | Mode | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical | Borrow existing infrastructure | Trackable traces |
| Network | Redundant, decentralized | Observable as system behavior |
| Carrier | Non-traditional media | Early experiment |

---

## Mid Extrapolation: Spatial Channels

GR allows discussion of wormhole- or warp-like geometries. They change causal path and distance; they are not everyday “freedom from light speed.” Traversable schemes are extreme and speculative.

If any spacetime shortcut were ever engineered, passing encoded states would usually remain closer to discussion than hauling matter. “Spatial escape” means shortening or reorganizing paths within law—not declaring information acausal. This layer is **not** required for the near-term three layers.

---

## Closing

Under definition D and selection: **information is not a passive trace; under suitable conditions it appears as a functional pattern with a tendency to continue.** On the near-term layers, carrier change and channel expansion are already discussable technical–civilizational facts; whether farther limit fantasies are possible remains open.

The claim stops at seeing how replicable patterns change carriers—not writing untested extrapolation as myth about to happen.

---

## Appendix: Limit Thought Experiments (Not Main Claims)

**Ontological escape:** no macroscopic material carrier; only energy configurations or field excitations. No operable frame yet.

**Hierarchical escape:** if the cosmos is a simulation, migration from inside to outside. Depends entirely on that hypothesis; not in ordinary test.
