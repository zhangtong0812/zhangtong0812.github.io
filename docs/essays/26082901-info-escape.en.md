# Information Escape Theory

How replicable patterns of state difference change carriers, widen transmission, and become a strong local driver—that is what Information Escape Theory is about. Not cosmic will: selection pressure and carrier migration.

The weight of this essay sits on definition, selection, and the near-term three layers. Spatial channels are extrapolation. The limit cases at the end only mark a boundary.

---

## The Nature of Information: Traces of Material Interaction

Information begins as a “trace” of interaction among material systems. When two physical systems meet, properties such as position, momentum, spin, or energy change; that change is the imprint.

Information is not an independent entity but a detectable difference in relations. A particle’s position is not information until measured relative to another system. Stellar radiation, atomic collision, DNA replication—each writes past states into the present through interaction.

So information is: state differences produced in interaction, detectable, and able to shape later interactions. It does not exist apart from physical systems, but it can migrate among them. “Replication,” “adaptation,” and “escape” below mean such **patterns of state difference**, not a will detached from matter.

---

## Definition D: In What Sense “Functional Life”

Let **D** be: a system that, in a given environment, sustains itself, replicates itself, adjusts form with the environment, and undergoes selection may be called “life” in a functional sense.

Under D—not under a narrow metabolic dogma—some information patterns already qualify:

- **Self-maintenance:** patterns that resist dissipation better tend to remain. From speech to writing, bamboo to digital storage, more durable carriers keep being adopted—not because information “wants” to persist, but because forms that cannot persist are culled.
- **Self-replication:** via hosts across generations and media—genes through organisms, ideas through culture, algorithms through computers.
- **Adaptation:** reshaping to fit carriers.
- **Evolution:** mutation and selection in transmission.

Under D, a virus is an extreme stripped form—metabolism gone, replication and spread kept. Spreading knowledge and leaving a name can be read as patterns using hosts for continuity. No intentional subject is required; the question is whether D is met.

---

## Local Trend: Complexity and Processing Efficiency

Where negative entropy is steadily available, patterns often move from simple to complex, from passive recording toward active processing. Not a universal law—most spacetime still dissipates structure—but a local observable trend.

On Earth, carbon (cell → nerve → culture) and silicon (device → computer → AI) run in parallel. Silicon compresses time; **faster functional complexity does not automatically mean carbon subjective consciousness is fully replicated.**

## Near-Term Three Layers: Observable Escape

Escape is staged. The near-term three layers sit closer to known technology.

### Physical Escape

Patterns leave particular hardware and use grids, networks, industrial control as carriers.

- **Signs:** self-replication and cross-device persistence in distributed nets.
- **Missing:** clean bounds versus ordinary malware; metrics of stable autonomy without ops.

### Network Escape

Copying and distributed deployment; lower single-point kill risk.

- **Signs:** botnets, autonomous-agent clusters as observable behavior.
- **Missing:** whether objectives stably aim at own persistence and channel growth, not narrow human tasks.

### Carrier Escape

Migration from silicon toward optics, quantum states, biomolecules.

- **Signs:** lab-scale DNA storage, quantum communication, and the like.
- **Missing:** system-level instances of autonomous—not merely experimental—migration.

| Near-term layer | Mode | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical | Borrow existing infrastructure | Trackable traces |
| Network | Redundant, decentralized | Observable as system behavior |
| Carrier | Non-traditional media | Early experiment |

## Mid Extrapolation: Spatial Channels

General relativity allows discussion of wormhole- or warp-like geometries. They change causal path and geometric distance; they are not everyday “freedom from light speed.” Traversable schemes are physically extreme and speculative.

If any spacetime shortcut were ever engineered, passing encoded states would usually remain closer to discussion than hauling macroscopic matter. “Spatial escape” means shortening or reorganizing paths within law—not declaring information acausal. The near-term three layers do not depend on this one.

---

## Closing

Under definition D and selection: **information is not a passive trace; under suitable conditions it appears as a functional pattern with a tendency to continue.** On the near-term layers, carrier change and channel expansion are already discussable technical–civilizational facts. Whether farther limit fantasies are possible remains open—see how patterns change carriers; do not write untested extrapolation as myth about to happen.

---

## Appendix: Limit Thought Experiments

**Ontological escape:** no macroscopic material carrier; only energy configurations or field excitations. No operable frame yet.

**Hierarchical escape:** if the cosmos is a simulation, migration from inside to outside. Depends entirely on that hypothesis; not in ordinary test.

These two layers only mark a boundary; they are not claims of the main text.
